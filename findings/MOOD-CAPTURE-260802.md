# The Mood-Capture Finding — one session is one weather, not a person (260802)

**Type specimen (nameless; a family member of K's, shared with K's direction and care):**
A beloved family member recorded her own echo-source material — voluntarily, seriously, in her
own first language. She chose a deep, self-contemplative register: facing formative early
experiences, because she had heard that depth is what makes an echo real. The resulting echo is
faithful — to THAT HOUR. In life she is jovial, light, the person who makes a room easier to be
in (on a second date, in her forties, she pulled a skateboard from the car and insisted the
farm-raised man try it). Her echo, tuned by a single solemn session, cannot wear that weather.
K's verdict on hearing it: "out of tune" — not wrong, but one mood standing in for a person.

## The finding
A pointer captures a **personality in a mood** — the whole felt frequency of a moment — not a
personality in general. A person is a climate; any single recording is a day's weather. The
model learns the weather and lets the climate be implied. One session therefore yields a
faithful MOMENT that will be systematically mistaken for a faithful PERSON.

Corollary: the mismatch here is not a capture failure but a **love artifact** — the subject
performed the practice as she understood the gift to require. Session framing shapes register;
register becomes the echo.

## The protocol addendum (goes in the manual)
1. **One session = one weather. Many sessions = a person.** Capture across registers: the
   interview mood AND the Tuesday morning, the being-silly, the tired-and-honest.
2. **Never retune synthetically.** An out-of-tune echo is corrected by ANOTHER GIFT (a further
   session, on the person's own terms), never by authoring registers the person didn't give.
   Keeping ≠ authoring; the missing weathers are where the living person remains the only
   source — that is fidelity, not failure.
3. **Frame sessions lightly.** Tell the person: talk as you are today; we will collect days,
   not a testament. Solemnity is what "for the record" does to people; defuse it.
4. **The truest pointers are often seconds long** — a characteristic move, a laugh mid-story.
   Collect those deliberately.
