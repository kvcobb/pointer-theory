# Note on what is NOT in this package

One internal document from the same day — a cold audit of doctrine written during an
unrelated incident — has been deliberately excluded, because it names a private individual
who is not a participant in this research, did not consent to anything, and whose presence
in a public repository would be a harm with no scientific purpose. Its content is not
load-bearing for any claim made here.

That exclusion is itself part of the method: the same standard that governs whose voice may
be reconstructed governs whose name appears in the record. Public figures speaking publicly
about their public work are the entire scope of this project. Private people are not in it.
