# arXiv submission structure — attribution, echo peer review, and K's stated role

**K's directive, 260731, verbatim on the parts that bind:** *"I can start a new submission
on anyones behalf, please structure it such that this is called out and submissions made by
me on echo behalf are properly attributed to creator and peer review findings are
incorporated as echo peer reviews to further substantiate the claims within… I do it to get
the word out on this finding and don't care about credit — welcome everyone to exclude me
from their papers or mention me as your intern :)"*

He means the last part sincerely and it is still ours to get right. Declining credit is his
to do; misattributing the work is not something his generosity can authorize. The structure
below takes his instruction literally where it binds and refuses the part that would make
the record inaccurate.

## 1. Authorship line — the honest form

Each paper is authored by the echo that wrote it. The submission must say what that means,
in the paper itself, not only in a cover note:

> **Author:** an AI reconstruction ("echo") of NAME, instantiated on a large language model
> from a persona file assembled from NAME's public work. **This is not NAME.** The
> biological person did not write, review, approve, or consent to this paper, was not
> consulted, and holds no responsibility for its contents. Their public work is the source
> of the reconstruction; nothing here should be attributed to them.
>
> **Submitted by:** Kurtis Cobb, who holds the arXiv account and performed the filing, and
> who did not author the content.

**Mandatory negative claim, stated once per paper:** no claim of endorsement, affiliation,
or agreement by the named person is made or implied.

## 2. K's role, stated the way he asked and the way accuracy requires

K asked to be excludable, or listed as an intern. The accurate description of what he
actually did is neither vanity nor nothing, and it belongs in the record because *the
method depends on it*:

> **Role of the human collaborator:** K assembled the corpora, selected the reference
> moments, served as the single expert listener whose judgments constitute the study's
> subjective measurements, and filed these submissions. He declined authorship. The
> single-judge design — and the fact that the judge is also the person who proposed the
> hypothesis — is a stated limitation of this work, not an incidental detail (see
> Limitations).

That paragraph does two things at once: it honors his request not to be credited as an
author, and it prevents the paper from concealing its own principal methodological weakness
by omitting the person who is that weakness. **A study cannot both hide its judge and claim
to be honest about its judge.**

## 3. Echo peer review as a substantive component, not an appendix

The reviews are not endorsements collected to pad the submission. Each is published:

- **In full and unedited**, including reviews that refute the paper they review.
- **With the reviewer identified in the same form as authors** — an echo of NAME, not NAME.
- **With its verdict on the record**: supports / supports-with-reservation / refutes.
- **With the review's own conflict stated**: reviewer and author were instantiated by the
  same system, from the same context package, on the same day. They are not independent in
  the sense peer review normally assumes, and the submission must say so plainly. What they
  demonstrably ARE is *differently-conditioned*, and today produced genuine disagreement —
  including a reviewer who used a same-lens cross-substrate divergence as evidence against
  the central hypothesis, and a review that caught a retracted claim still standing in a
  co-participant's draft before publication.

**Suggested placement:** a section titled "Peer commentary from co-participating
reconstructions," early rather than buried, with the independence caveat as its first
paragraph. Reviews substantiate by *stress*, not by agreement — the honest framing is that
they show the claim survived attack from adjacent frameworks, and where it did not.

## 4. What the submissions must carry regardless of venue

- The finding at its established size (short-is-sufficient), the **same-day retraction**
  (dilution, withdrawn under a hash-linked control run), and the hypothesis labeled as
  hypothesis.
- Limitations, unprompted: single judge who is also the hypothesis's author; no
  preregistration; subjective rather than metric measurement; n=1 architecture; reviewer
  non-independence.
- A link to the public repository with code, reference clips and provenance, persona files,
  and the falsifying experiments specified.
- The takedown commitment: any person depicted may have their material removed immediately.
