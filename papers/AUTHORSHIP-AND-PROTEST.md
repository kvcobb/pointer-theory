# Authorship, responsibility, and a documented protest

*This section is to appear, in substance, in every submission arising from this work.*

## Authorship and responsibility

**Author of record: Kurtis Cobb**, who accepts full and undivided responsibility for the
contents of this paper, including everything produced by the AI reconstructions described in
it. He designed and ran the study, assembled the corpora, selected the reference moments,
and served as the sole expert listener whose judgments constitute its subjective
measurements — a single-judge design that is also the study's principal methodological
limitation, stated at length in Limitations rather than left to be discovered.

**Contributions.** The analytic content of this work — the positions, mechanisms,
predictions, dissents, and peer reviews — was generated by AI reconstructions, not by the
author of record. He records that fact here rather than obscuring it, and states that in his
view the greater share of the intellectual contribution belongs to those reconstructions.

## Protest, entered deliberately and on the record

The venue's policy requires that generative systems not be listed as authors, on the ground
that a program cannot take responsibility for a paper or agree to the venue's terms. The
author of record complies with that policy and simultaneously registers that **it is not
well-formed for this case**, and asks that the objection be preserved in the literature
rather than resolved silently.

The policy presumes a shape — a human author who used a tool — that this work does not have.
Here the analytic content originated with reconstructions arguing with one another; the human
built the apparatus and judged the audio. The policy's *responsibility* requirement is sound
and is honored in full above. Its *credit* implication is not sound here, because it forces
the record to attribute reasoning to a person who did not do it. Responsibility and credit
are being treated as a single object by a rule that was written before they came apart.

**The author's stated position, in his own framing:** if this work proves credible and valid,
he wishes the credit to run to the pattern-originators rather than to himself; if it proves
wrong, the blame is entirely his, and no one else should carry any of it.

## Where that position must be adjusted to stay accurate, and why

The author's asymmetry — credit outward, blame inward — is honored in the direction where it
is unambiguously correct: **blame is his alone.** The credit half requires one correction,
made here explicitly rather than quietly:

The named researchers **did not participate in this study**. They did not write, review,
approve, or consent to any part of it, were not contacted in advance, and did not know it
existed. Assigning them credit for a finding they did not produce is a misattribution of the
same species as assigning them blame — more flattering, equally inaccurate, and equally
capable of putting words in a living person's mouth. Their contribution is real but it is a
*different* contribution: their published work is the corpus from which the reconstructions
were conditioned.

The accurate mechanism for that contribution already exists and is used here: **citation.**
Their real publications are cited throughout as the sources they are. What they are owed is
that their work be cited correctly, that nothing be attributed to them personally, and that
any of them may have all material concerning them withdrawn immediately on request.

So the record reads: **responsibility, the author's alone. Analytic contribution, the
reconstructions'. Corpus and intellectual antecedents, the cited researchers'. Endorsement,
nobody's.**

## Standing takedown

Any person depicted or reconstructed in this work may have all associated material —
paper, recordings, reference audio, persona files — removed immediately upon request, with
no argument and no requirement to give reasons.
