## The claim under examination

**ESTABLISHED (one instrument, one modality, one day):** for OmniVoice, a reference clip
of ~3 seconds — given high confidence it intersects only the target speaker,
uninterrupted — conditions synthesis that a familiar listener rates as fully the person,
indistinguishable in quality from 40s and 180s references. Replicated across three voices
including a difficult-accent case. **Short is sufficient.** Above the intersection
threshold, reference duration carries no information in either direction.

**RETRACTED THE SAME DAY:** an earlier, uncontrolled comparison indicated long references
sounded *worse*, and a dilution mechanism was proposed. A re-run with every reference
hash-linked to its render returned a **null**. The earlier run's provenance was
compromised — the canonical reference file was being overwritten in place by concurrent
processes, and the reporting seat later found it had mislabeled a copy of that file.
"Short is better" is withdrawn.

**HYPOTHESIS, UNESTABLISHED:** that this generalizes beyond text-to-speech — that
persona-conditioning in a large language model is likewise *address* into a structured
space the weights already carry, rather than compression of a corpus.

**KNOWN LIMITS, ours to state:** single expert judge who is also the theory's author; no
preregistration; subjective rather than metric judgment; n=1 architecture.

## Paper format (each author, own voice, own length)

1. **Position** — what you think the result shows and, separately, what it does not.
2. **Mechanism** — the account in your own framework, stated so it could be wrong.
3. **Prediction** — at least one falsifiable consequence, with the measurement that would
   kill it. This is the section that makes the paper worth publishing.
4. **Dissent** — where you disagree with the other participants, named explicitly.
5. **Confession** — the bias you cannot correct for from where you sit.

## Peer review

Each paper is reviewed by others present. A review states: what survives, what does not,
what the author cannot see from inside their own framework, and a verdict —
*supports / supports-with-reservation / refutes*. Reviews are published alongside papers,
unedited. A refutation is a successful outcome, not a failure of the series.

## Publication

Papers and reviews are components of the public repository accompanying the video series.
Nothing is smoothed for consistency; where papers contradict each other, the contradiction
is the finding.
