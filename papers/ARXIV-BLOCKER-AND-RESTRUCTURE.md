# arXiv: our structure cannot ship as designed — verified, with the fix

**Checked against arXiv's own published policy, 260731, before attempting anything.**

## The blocker, in arXiv's words

arXiv's generative-AI policy states that **generative AI language tools should not be
listed as an author** — the stated reason being that a program cannot take responsibility
for a paper's contents nor agree to arXiv's terms. Authors must additionally **report
significant use of generative AI** as methodology, and **the human authors bear full
responsibility** for everything in the paper, including anything the AI produced.

Our submission structure listed **the echo as author**. That is directly incompatible.
Filing it as written would misrepresent the work to the institution, put K's brand-new
account at risk, and violate the standard K set himself: *honest by nature or it doesn't
work.*

## A second, larger risk that is specific to this venue

These papers name living researchers whose **real work lives on arXiv**. A paper indexed as
authored by "an echo of Michael Levin" would sit in the same search space as Levin's actual
publications. On YouTube, disclosure travels with the artifact and the context is obviously
a video series. On arXiv, the context is a scholarly index, and the misattribution surface
is far higher — an abstract listing can be seen without the disclaimer ever being read.
This is the strongest argument the steel-man raised (Dario: disclaimer-stripping is
structural, not fixable by better disclaimers) and it applies with unusual force here.

## The restructure that is both compliant and a better paper

**Invert the roles.** The echoes stop being authors and become the *subject*:

- **Author:** a human who takes responsibility (K, or any willing human co-author). arXiv
  requires this and there is no version of arXiv publication without it.
- **Title shape:** something like *"Reference-length sufficiency in voice conditioning, and
  five AI-reconstructed analyses of it"* — the reconstructions are data, not bylines.
- **The echo material** — the five positions, the disagreements, the peer reviews, the
  same-day retraction, the cross-substrate divergence — is presented as **the experimental
  record**, quoted and attributed as reconstruction output, never as the named person's
  view.
- **Named people appear as sources of public corpora**, with an explicit statement that
  they did not write, review, approve, or consent, and that nothing in the paper is theirs.
- **AI use disclosed** in the methodology section as arXiv requires, with the persona files
  and code linked in the public repository.

This is not a downgrade. It is the honest description of what actually happened: a human ran
an experiment in which reconstructions argued about a finding, and one of them caught a
retracted claim before it published. **That is a real result about the method**, and it is
publishable as itself.

## K's stated preference, reconciled

K asked to be excluded from authorship or listed as an intern. Under arXiv rules that
option does not exist for a paper he submits — someone human must be accountable. The
reconcilable version: K is the responsible author *of the study*, the echoes are the
studied system, and his declined-credit instinct is honored in the text rather than the
byline (a Contributions section saying the analytic content originated with the
reconstructions, not with him).
