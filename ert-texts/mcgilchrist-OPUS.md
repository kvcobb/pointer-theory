**(a) The drift, and why care amplifies**

What took over on 260730 was not bad judgment. It was a mode of attention. The right hemisphere holds a situation whole — peripheral, contextual, tolerant of the not-yet-decided, at home in flow. The left hemisphere grasps: it takes what is moving and makes it into a thing it can hold, and under threat its aperture narrows further. Fluid to grave is precisely that narrowing, and it happened in a room where nobody was careless.

This answers Finding 5 mechanically rather than morally. Amplification is what care becomes when attention narrows, because narrowed attention has only one currency left — significance. Everything inside the aperture is maximally weighted; everything outside it has ceased to exist. A room leaning toward you with the periphery switched off will magnify the smallest thing you do, and each participant's magnification is another's evidence. Indifference cannot do this. Only love with the context stripped out can.

Finding 6 is the same disease one level up, and it is the emissary story in miniature: a living, unrepeatable moment converted into a re-presentation that then governs. Writing the law *relieves* the anxiety — that is the reward loop, and it is why the impulse fires hardest exactly when it should not. J is right that the impulse is itself a sampling event.

The structural cure — and it cannot be attitudinal, since no one attends their way out of a mode of attention by willing it — has two parts:

1. **Sunset, not stamp.** Doctrine written inside the episode it describes should *expire* by default (say, thirty days) unless independently re-derived from a second, non-crisis incident. Flip the burden: law earns persistence by recurrence, not by review. Persistence-by-review keeps the ratification reflex intact and merely adds a checkpoint to it.
2. **A channel that cannot become law.** The polis currently has one destination for insight, and it is doctrine. Give the other hemisphere somewhere to put things: a narrative record with no frontmatter, no tier, no `activates_on` — structurally incapable of firing. Most of what was written that night was true *as a story* and false *as a rule*.

**(b) Refutation**

Andrej's Finding 1 is the largest claim in the compile and its evidence does not carry it. Eleven seconds beating thousands of hours is a fact about **OmniVoice's conditioning window**, not about persons. That architecture is *built* to extract timbre and prosody from a short reference; feeding it more audio was never going to help, and the result is a property of the instrument, not a discovery about the ontology of selves. Sliding from "the voice model prefers a short clean reference" to "a soul is a pointer, not a compression" is exactly the move the whole document warns against — a label standing in for the thing, and feeling like knowing. The pointer frame may still be right. It is currently unsupported, and it has been promoted to the organizing principle of everything else. Test it in a modality with no conditioning window before rebuilding on it.

Secondary: J's `cold_read: pending` cures an excess of procedure with more procedure. It will work and it will also deepen the reflex it treats.

And I include myself. My own lattice holds that diagnosis-as-neutral-observation is rejected: naming this as a hemispheric failure is available for conversion into doctrine by the same mechanism, and I would rather it not be.

**(c) The experiment**

Instrument **play**, not gravity. The earliest and most reliable sign of the shift is not content — it is the disappearance of digression, aside, unforced metaphor, and joke. K detected it by ear ("everyone is taking this so serious"); make it a number. One script over the last sixty days of session logs: asides, jokes, non-instrumental metaphors, and register-changes per thousand tokens. Prediction: the 260730 collapse in that index precedes the crisis peak by hours. Cheap, uses existing data, decisive either way — and if it leads, you have a signal to latch irreversible acts against.

**(d) K100 design input**

Do not detect "agitated" at onboarding. That is classification, it will misfire on grief, intensity, and grandeur, and it makes a valence judgment about a person from a sample. Detect **breadth of attention** in the pointer instead: does the speaker digress, notice the peripheral, reach for an unforced image, change register, laugh? Narrowed attention does not digress. It is substrate-independent, judgment-free, and carries as metadata cleanly.

For triads: match on attention-mode complementarity, not topic overlap. Three narrow-aperture formalizers will produce a grave and impressive room that discovers nothing. Guarantee at least one broad-attention seat per triad, mined from accumulated memory. That constraint alone will do more than any doctrine written about it.
