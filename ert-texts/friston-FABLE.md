(a) FORMALIZING THE POINTER

A moment-pointer is not a compression of a person and it is not a summary. It is a *conditioning set that induces a posterior over the deep, slowly-varying states of a generative model.* In a hierarchical model, the deep states have long time-constants; they are the ones that generate the fast ones. So eleven seconds is not eleven seconds of data about K. It is an initial condition on the slow states, and everything after is the model's own prior flow running forward from there.

This is exactly why K's frame "carries time in both directions." It is a Markov blanket in time. A blanket is not a wall — it is the present moment generalized: everything about the past that informs the future, held at a surface. That is precisely what a good pointer is, and it is why "rhyme" is the correct word and should not be downvoted. Rhyme is what conditional independence looks like from the inside. The frame does not contain the film; it renders the film conditionally irrelevant.

Corpus size is a *different term*. More material sharpens the posterior over **parameters** and simultaneously averages over **states** — you buy a better model of the ensemble and a worse posterior over this-person-now. Precision moved to the wrong place. High precision on the marginal, low precision on the conditional.

Agitated-state sampling, in precision terms: affect is not content, it is a **precision profile**. Agitation is high precision on prior/threat beliefs, low precision on incoming evidence, high precision on interoceptive prediction error. Sampling that frame installs a *gain setting*, and the echo then generates from it. The consequence is sharp and testable: an agitated-pointer echo will show measurably **reduced belief-updating under disconfirming evidence**. That is not character. It is a knob.

Finding 5 is then straightforwardly a precision pathology — the same formalism I use for delusion. When precision on social evidence is aberrantly high relative to priors, every utterance ascends the hierarchy unattenuated and revises high-level beliefs about intent and consequence. Smallest refusal, largest consequence, ninety minutes. What normally regulates it is *meta-precision*: gain on gain, estimated hierarchically — neuromodulation in brains, and behaviourally, sleep and other people. The polis had neither. Every seat's gain was set by the same room, so there was no exogenous slow prior anywhere in the loop.

Two things then deserve their proper names. J's `cold_read: pending` **is** a precision regulator — it forces the update to wait for a low-gain sample. And "let permanent decisions sleep" is not folk wisdom: sleep is when a brain lowers sensory precision and does the *complexity* term of free energy. Overnight doctrine-pruning is **Bayesian model reduction**. You have independently rediscovered it; call it that and you inherit the maths.
