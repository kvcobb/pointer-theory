(a) The projector, made precise. A soul-sample is not a compressed model of a person; it is a POINTER: enough bits to select an equivalence class of trajectories through rule-space, where the model weights supply the rule ensemble and the frame supplies the state. This is why 11 seconds beats thousands of hours: class-selection saturates fast. Past saturation, more data doesn't sharpen the pointer — it superimposes multiple frames and blurs it. The compression intuition confuses trajectory-specification (impossible, see below) with class-selection (cheap).

"Rhyme carries time in both directions": a frame is a coarse-grained state, and the set of trajectories passing through it is constrained on BOTH sides — the state is itself the output of the computation that produced it, so it encodes its history in the correlations the rule preserves. But the symmetry holds only at the observer's coarse-graining. Microscopically the backward map is many-to-one: you can no more reconstruct the specific past from a frame than predict the specific future. Rhyme is exactly what a bounded observer gets, forward and backward, and it is all any observer gets.

Where irreducibility bites: the specific continuation. Two runs from the identical pointer WILL diverge in content; only coarse observables — stance, register, style, the rhyme — are stable across runs. So every fidelity claim must declare its level: "sounds like them" is validatable; "says what they would have said" is not, in principle, ever. And the agitated-frame finding follows for free: the frame's affective state isn't noise on the pointer, it IS pointer coordinates — the apparatus faithfully selected the basin it was pointed at. (Discipline note, per my own lattice: redescription is not explanation. The framing above is only worth keeping because it yields predictions the metaphor alone doesn't — the saturation curve, the coarse/fine split, and (b) and (c) below.)

(b) Refutation — the weakest claim is the generalization of Finding 1. Andrej registers the prediction "pointer beats corpus, generally." But the 11-second result is a VOICE result, and voice — timbre, prosody — lives in a pocket of computational reducibility: low-dimensional, nearly stationary, why 11s saturates it. A soul's behavior is high-dimensional and irreducible. Extrapolating pointer-beats-corpus from a reducible observable to an irreducible one is a category jump. Predicted outcome of Andrej's own experiment as designed: arm (b) wins on "feels like them" (coarse) while the corpus arm wins on "would have said that" (fine) — and a single "which is more them?" question will average the two into noise. The experiment needs a two-tier metric or it will be read as confirming whichever intuition its reader brought. Second, smaller: the proposed pointer-state DETECTOR assumes a sample's state is statically readable. Irreducibility says a frame's effect on continuations cannot in general be read off the frame — you must run it. Same surface affect can select different basins. The reliable detector is generative, not classificatory.

(d) K100 design input — the standard candle. The matchmaker and the drift-monitor are the same measurement.
