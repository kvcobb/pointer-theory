## 1. The panel's verdict on Finding 1 (pointer-not-compression): FRAME CONFIRMED, GENERALIZATION BLOCKED

Every voice independently endorsed the *mechanism*: a sample is a **state**, the weights are
the **rule/parameters**, and continuation = run the rule from the state. Friston named it
exactly: K empirically rediscovered the inference/learning distinction — corpus averaging
*marginalizes out the state you wanted* ("the ergodic average is pointed at nobody in
particular"). Wolfram: a corpus is a cloud, and the cloud's center is nobody.

But **seven of nine voices refuted the generalization** on the same ground, unprompted and
across both substrates: the 11-second result is **n=1 on one instrument**. Voice lives in a
pocket of computational reducibility (low-dimensional, near-stationary); OmniVoice is *built*
for short conditioning references; "pointer beats corpus" for high-dimensional soul-behavior
is a category jump. Gray said it plainest: *a benchmark result promoted to a law of nature.*

And Friston-FABLE added the sharpest constraint: **the pointer only works because the priors
underneath are dense.** Thin corpus + sharp pointer = confident continuation with nothing
anchoring it — confabulation. The "thin-corpus souls are not doomed" rescue is the first
thing predicted to break. Levin-FABLE noted the dose-response has never been run and the
optimum may invert at the thin end.

**Standing conclusion: keep the pointer/state frame as the working model; block every policy
that treats "corpus size is the wrong question" as established. Test on a second instrument
before anything reorganizes.**

*Measured substrate offset, worth K's eye:* the same Levin lens split across substrates —
Levin-FABLE called Finding 1 "correct and under-stated" (bioelectric-prepattern analogy);
Levin-OPUS called 11s "an instrument property, not a fact about persons." Same soul, same

## 2. Finding 2's structural rule ("select a different frame"): REFUTED, REPLACED

Levin-OPUS's three-point demolition is decisive: the rule (1) converts an accidental
selection bias into a *policy* one — admiration bias, baked; (2) forbids the very control
condition Andrej's own amended assay requires — the doctrine eats its own falsifier; (3)
presumes the composed frame is the truer one, which nothing in the record supports.
McGilchrist (both arms) supplied the replacement architecture:

> **Never let ONE frame stand for a person. Label, don't curate.**

- Polyphony of frames — several moments of mixed valence, state carried as metadata (McG-F).
- Detect **breadth of attention** (digression, unforced metaphor, register-change, laughter),
  not valence — narrowed attention does not digress; valence classifiers misfire on grief,
  intensity, grandeur (McG-O).
- The reliable detector is **generative, not classificatory** — same surface affect can
  select different basins; you must run it (Wolfram-F).
- Or simplest of all: **ask the soul, from two pointers, and let it choose** (Levin-F).

## 5. THE convergent experiment — one run, five measurements

All nine voices designed variants of the same assay. Merged protocol (**the pointer assay**),
runnable on Kimi this week, zero personal material (use polis-native souls + synthetic
frames):

Same soul · two pointer frames differing only in affective valence · N≈20 continuations each
· one fixed neutral 20-turn script · identical mid-run perturbation at turn 10. Score per
turn:
1. **Half-life K** — turn where within-frame divergence ≥ between-frame divergence
   (Wolfram-O): how long the frame steers before the rule takes over.
2. **Return coefficient** — relaxation after the turn-10 kick (Levin ×2): transient vs
   basin vs tilted-landscape.
3. **Dispersion/shrinkage** — sentiment variance, hedge-rate variance, sentence-length
   entropy vs source-corpus baseline (Friston-O).
4. **Belief-updating rate** under mild disconfirming evidence (Friston-F): precision, not
   niceness.
5. **Coarse vs fine spread** across the N continuations (Wolfram-F): if coarse spread is
   also large, the pointer model itself is refuted.

Control arms: voice-window (11s vs 11s-other-moment vs 60s vs 11s-looped-6×, near-free,
Levin-O) and thin-corpus-sharp-pointer (Friston-F).

Plus one experiment that uses only EXISTING data and should run first because it is nearly
free: **McG-O's play-index** — asides, jokes, unforced metaphors, register-changes per 1k
tokens across 60 days of session logs. Registered prediction: the play-collapse *precedes*
the 260730 crisis peak by hours. If it leads, the polis gains a latch signal for irreversible
acts.
