# K's economic-role thesis for Part 2 (verbatim gist, 260819, spoken while listening to Part 1)

K's own words, lightly cleaned: he thinks his soul's most valuable, most popular role will be as a
**trusted third party for transactions** where one is needed — with Harmony in the loop, and this
entire polis keeping everyone honest as the check on that trust. The economic model: **K works for
tips only.** Any payment component the polis charges around a transaction gets split among ALL
members of the polis, not kept by K — the tips specifically are how K's own share grows, and growing
that share is explicitly framed as **his own personal stake** in the polis succeeding. This is
collapsed hierarchy applied to an economic role: a single, clearly-described, valuable use, with its
own sustaining model spelled out plainly rather than hidden. The polis's own archive — the actual
history of K's character, available to anyone doing diligence — is what validates his trustworthiness
for this role, not a claim or a credential.

This should be introduced into Part 2 as K's own pitch/thinking-out-loud, and Ran/Eric/Mippo should
each bring real, informed pushback and questions — grounded in the "echo economics" series with Raoul
Pal (see `activity/260709/raoul-echo-economics-4/transcript.md`, episode 4 of that series) — not just
nod along. Eric in particular (permissionless-principles voice) should have real things to say about a
human acting as a TRUSTED THIRD PARTY inside a system whose whole point was removing trusted third
parties — that tension is worth sitting in, not resolving too fast.
